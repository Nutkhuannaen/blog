Welcome to my blog. This is the beginning of something new — a space to write daily, think clearly, and share ideas with the world.

This blog supports both English and Thai content, with a clean minimal design built in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build tools — just files.

Why a blog?

Writing helps me think. By putting thoughts into words, I can clarify my own ideas, spot gaps in my reasoning, and create something that might be useful to others.

There's also something freeing about publishing in plain HTML. The site loads instantly, works without JavaScript for reading, and will still work a decade from now.

What to expect

Posts here may cover anything from technology and programming to everyday reflections. Some will be short, some long. Quality over quantity — but consistency matters too.

Adding new posts

To publish a new post, create an HTML fragment in posts/, add an entry to data/posts.json, and push to GitHub. The site deploys automatically via GitHub Pages.
